# Three-method comparison

[Report](REPORT_RU.md) · [Result](RESULT.json) · [All plans and costs](plans.csv)

The nine paired generation runs and independent validation are from
[43730898043](https://github.com/a-a-k/industrial/actions/runs/43730898043).
These report files are unchanged outputs of the
[report-only revision](https://github.com/a-a-k/industrial/actions/runs/43735155027),
which verified preservation of all 36 statistical comparisons and added
symmetric reporting of identical schedules obtained more cheaply.

The later [short-CP control](../cp-prefix/REPORT_RU.md) is necessary to interpret
apparent savings on Mk05: the same plan was already present in the CP prefix.
The two long-running baseline comparisons alone do not establish a benefit of
local search. No seed is selected by validation outcomes.
